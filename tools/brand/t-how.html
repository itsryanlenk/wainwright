THE TRAIL TO A ONE-JOB BOT

1 ASK

Only what a test cannot settle: job, name and voice, wake, who it talks to. Then wait.

2 DRAFT

One job. Anti-jobs. Voice. Wake. In that order, nothing extra.

3 LINT

14 checks. One fail and the wagon does not leave.

4 CREATE

CreateAgent with the linted text. No delete, so no second try.

5 VERIFY

Read profile.json. The tool saying "done" does not count.

6 HAND OFF

Routine sent with SendToAgent. Logged after the bot confirms.
